## Applied

- The actual FurYou and WHELPS image logos replace the provisional paw wordmark. A cocoa backing preserves the original white lettering.
- Ivory, warm taupe, cocoa, and orange replace the provisional green identity. Fraunces provides a similar editorial character, with Nunito Sans retained for readable mobile controls.
- The original reference cat appears in a portrait crop with contrast scrim. The welcome heading follows “Find your pawrfect companion.”
- Mobile navigation, discovery and filters, favorites, adoption assessment, activity, profile, rehoming, and rescue remain available.
- Our Story opens a scrollable sheet. The adoption journey condenses browse, account/assessment, review, meeting, and welcome-home concepts from the reference.
- Photo scale settles over 1.4 seconds, entrances are staggered, and touch feedback settles in 160ms. Decorative motion honors reduced-motion preferences and does not loop continuously.

## Integration boundaries

The reference also describes live WHELPS messaging, screening decisions, post-adoption monitoring, donations, and Google login. These require backend/provider integration and are not represented as functioning services by this visual update. Existing local drafts and accounts remain local. Reference statistics and testimonials were not copied into the live UI as verified outcomes. Existing sample pets are explicitly labeled on Home.

Original logo downloads were the rendered preview assets (FurYou 150×48). Use owner-provided full-resolution files for production/store branding. Existing native launcher icons remain provisional; this update changes in-app branding.
